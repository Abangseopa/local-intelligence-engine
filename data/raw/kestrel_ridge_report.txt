Technical Report: Kestrel Ridge Lithium-Cobalt Project

1. Overview

The Kestrel Ridge Project is a critical-minerals mining operation located in the
Whitlock Basin, approximately 42 kilometers northeast of the town of Amberfield.
The site is operated by Northgate Resources under exploration permit WB-2291 and
mining lease ML-7734, issued by the regional Department of Mines in March 2021.
The project targets a combined lithium and cobalt deposit hosted in pegmatite
intrusions, with construction completed in late 2023 and commercial production
beginning in February 2024.

2. Location and Geology

The Kestrel Ridge deposit sits within a Precambrian pegmatite belt that also hosts
tantalum and beryl mineralization. Drilling since 2019 has outlined a mineralized
zone extending roughly 3.1 kilometers along strike and averaging 45 meters in
width. The spodumene-bearing pegmatites are the primary lithium ore, while cobalt
occurs as a secondary product within associated sulfide veins.

3. Resource Estimates

The measured and indicated resource is estimated at 58.4 million tonnes at an
average grade of 1.24% lithium oxide (Li2O) and 0.09% cobalt. Proven reserves
support a projected mine life of 19 years at current extraction rates. An
independent audit conducted in September 2023 confirmed the resource estimate
using the standard reporting code for mineral disclosures.

4. Extraction and Processing

Ore is extracted using open-pit bench mining with 10-meter benches. Run-of-mine
ore is crushed, then concentrated through dense-media separation followed by
froth flotation to produce a spodumene concentrate averaging 5.8% Li2O. The
concentrate is trucked to a regional rail terminal 65 kilometers away for export.
Cobalt-bearing sulfides are recovered separately through a flotation circuit
installed in the second phase of the processing plant, commissioned in June 2024.

5. Environmental Safeguards

The site employs a dry-stack tailings storage facility rather than a conventional
tailings dam, reducing water usage and eliminating the risk of dam failure. Process
water is recycled at a rate of 87%, drawn primarily from a closed-loop circuit fed
by an on-site water treatment plant. A 2023 environmental impact assessment
identified the greater sage grouse as a species of concern, and a 1,200-hectare
offset habitat area has been established 8 kilometers south of the pit.

6. Workforce and Safety

The operation employs 340 full-time workers, of whom 61% reside in Amberfield or
surrounding communities. The site reported a lost-time injury frequency rate
(LTIFR) of 0.9 per million hours worked in 2024, below the industry benchmark of
1.6. All workers complete a mandatory five-day safety induction before entering
active mining areas.

7. Production Timeline and Outlook

Commercial production began in February 2024 with an initial output target of
180,000 tonnes of spodumene concentrate per year, ramping to a planned 260,000
tonnes per year by 2026 following completion of the second processing line.
Cobalt by-product output is forecast at 1,450 tonnes per year once the sulfide
flotation circuit reaches full capacity. Northgate Resources has stated that a
feasibility study for a downstream lithium hydroxide conversion facility near
Amberfield is expected to be completed by late 2027.
